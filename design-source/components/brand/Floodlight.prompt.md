Every EasyGame screen stands on this: a navy sky (deep at the top, lifting to blue) lit by two floodlight pools, with faint vertical pitch lines, meeting a pale ground. The app bar and screen headline live in the sky; glass panels start inside it and scroll down onto the ground.

```jsx
<Floodlight skyHeight={300} style={{ width: 390, height: 844 }}>…</Floodlight>
```

Never place opaque white blocks in the sky — only glass, dark glass or text.

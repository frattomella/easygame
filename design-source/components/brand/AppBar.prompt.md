The header is not a bar — it is text set directly in the sky, with the club or date as a tracked eyebrow and the section as a tight 800-weight title. Controls are dark glass Icon Chips.

```jsx
<AppBar eyebrow="AS Roma Giovanili · Mer 18 mar" title="Allenamenti" notificationCount={3} onNotifications={open} />
```

No logo in the header; the layered sky, the type and the chips are the brand.

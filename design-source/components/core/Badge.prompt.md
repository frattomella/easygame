The EasyGame **Status Pill**. The ring-dot is the tell: hollow ring = neutral taxonomy (category, role), filled ring = a live status. Labels are short Italian words set uppercase and tracked.

```jsx
<Badge label="Under 15" small />                       // hollow ring, neutral
<Badge label="Attivo" variant="success" small />       // filled green ring
<Badge label="Infortunato" variant="warning" small />
<Badge label="Gara" variant="match" small />
<Badge label="3" variant="onDark" small dot={false} />  // counter on the navy sky
```

Never a button; never more than two pills per row.
